# Luminary Press Visual Refresh and Hybrid Publishing

## What will change
- Refresh the entire site in the selected editorial boutique direction: charcoal, warm paper, crimson, and antique gold.
- Strengthen the typography with bold literary headlines, selective italic emphasis, and cleaner body text.
- Rework backgrounds, spacing, imagery, buttons, navigation, service presentation, and mobile layouts for a richer publishing-house feel.
- Remove every em dash from visible website copy.

## Hybrid publishing
- Add a dedicated **Publishing** page and include it in the main navigation.
- Present Luminary Press as a selective hybrid publisher with two connected strengths: curated publishing and strategic book growth.
- Show a complete journey from manuscript review through editing, design, production, publication, and launch.
- Add a manuscript review enquiry path using the existing enquiry system, without inventing prices, acceptance claims, timelines, distribution guarantees, or submission availability.
- Keep the free Book Growth Blueprint as the main action throughout the site.

- **Other pages:** apply the new visual system and remove em dashes without changing existing prices or business facts.
